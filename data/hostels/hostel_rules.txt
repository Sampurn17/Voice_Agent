Hostel Rules and Regulations – IIIT Una
========================================

General Rules:
1. Hostel accommodation is compulsory for all registered students unless exempted by the Dean (Student Affairs).
2. Room allotment is done by the Chief Warden's office at the beginning of each academic year. Students must not swap rooms without written permission.
3. Every hosteller must carry their institute ID card at all times within the campus and hostel premises.
4. Visitors are not permitted inside the hostel rooms. Meeting with visitors/parents is allowed only in the designated visitor's area during visiting hours (10:00 AM to 6:00 PM).
5. Students must maintain discipline and decorum in the hostel. Any form of ragging, bullying, or harassment is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate expulsion from the hostel and disciplinary action.

Hostel Timings:
- Hostel gates close at 10:00 PM during weekdays and 11:00 PM on weekends.
- Students must be present in the hostel during roll call, which is conducted at 10:30 PM by the hostel warden.
- Night-outs require prior written permission from the Warden. Only two night-outs per month are permitted, and a parent/guardian must authorize the request.

Mess Rules:
- The hostel mess is managed by a Mess Committee comprising elected student representatives and a Faculty Warden (Mess).
- Mess timings:
  - Breakfast: 7:30 AM to 9:00 AM
  - Lunch: 12:30 PM to 2:00 PM
  - Snacks: 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM
  - Dinner: 7:30 PM to 9:00 PM
- Students opting out of the mess for a semester must inform the Mess Committee at least 15 days before the semester begins. Partial opt-out is not allowed.
- Mess fee for the current semester: INR 22,000 (subject to review by the mess committee).

Electrical Appliances:
- Only low-power appliances such as phone chargers, laptop chargers, and study lamps are permitted in hostel rooms.
- Use of electric heaters, induction cooktops, irons, or any high-wattage appliance is strictly prohibited.
- Violation will result in confiscation of the appliance and a fine of INR 2,000.

Internet and Wi-Fi:
- High-speed Wi-Fi is available in all hostel blocks. Students connect using their institute credentials.
- Misuse of internet (accessing restricted content, torrenting, or any illegal activity) will result in suspension of internet privileges and disciplinary action.

Maintenance and Cleanliness:
- Housekeeping staff clean common areas daily. Students are responsible for maintaining cleanliness in their own rooms.
- Any damage to hostel property (furniture, fixtures, doors, windows) will be charged to the student(s) responsible.
- Complaints regarding plumbing, electrical, or furniture issues should be reported to the Hostel Caretaker or through the online complaint portal.

Leave and Vacation:
- During semester breaks and vacations, students must vacate the hostel unless they have written permission to stay from the Warden.
- Students staying during vacations must register with the Warden and follow all applicable rules.

Disciplinary Actions:
- First offence (minor): Written warning from the Warden.
- Second offence: Fine and temporary hostel suspension.
- Repeated or major offences: Permanent expulsion from the hostel and referral to the Institute Disciplinary Committee.
