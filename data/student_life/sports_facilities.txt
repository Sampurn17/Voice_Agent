Sports Facilities – IIIT Una

Source: IIIT Una Annual Progress Report 2023–24 and Student Activity Council Policy
Prepared: 30 October 2026
Purpose: Initial text source for the IIIT Una Voice Assistant RAG knowledge base.

Sports Grounds and Facilities (from Annual Progress Report 2023–24):
The IIIT Una Annual Progress Report 2023–24 lists the following sports facilities:
- Cricket ground.
- Basketball ground.
- Football ground.
- Athletic track.
- Volleyball ground.
- Lawn tennis.
- Multiple badminton grounds.
- Indoor sports room in each hostel.

Additional Fitness and Recreation Facilities:
The report describes:
- A dedicated music room.
- A well-equipped gymnasium for physical fitness.

Historical Reporting Note:
These sports and fitness details come from the 2023–24 Annual Progress Report and should be treated as historical institutional reporting until confirmed against current operational information.

Sports Training and Staff:
The report states that the institute has dedicated sports staff to train students in sports activities and facilitates student participation in inter-institutional sports events.

Sports and Recreational Society (SRS):
The Student Activity Council Policy describes the Sports and Recreational Society (SRS) as promoting physical fitness, recreation, teamwork, leadership, and community among students, faculty, and staff.
Its stated responsibilities include:
- Encouraging students to participate in sports of their choice.
- Administering day-to-day sports activities.
- Organizing the Annual Sports Meet.
- Conducting inter-hostel and inter-department tournaments each semester.
- Organizing inter-IIIT sports meets and other inter-institutional activities.
- Administering participation in national-level sports competitions.
- Organizing sports activities for freshers.
- Arranging sports seminars, workshops, and training camps.

Clubs under SRS:
The policy lists ZENITH and TRAVISTA under SRS. The official Travista club page describes Travista as the Travel and Tourism Club. Treat the policy's club grouping as a policy statement, and verify the current club directory before describing current organization structure.

Sports Events:
The SAC Policy refers to:
- Annual Sports Meet.
- Inter-hostel and inter-department tournaments.
- Inter-IIIT sports meets.
- National-level sports participation.
