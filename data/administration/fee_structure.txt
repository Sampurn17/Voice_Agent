Fee Structure – IIIT Una (Academic Year 2026-27)
=================================================

B.Tech Program Fee (Per Semester):

1. Tuition Fee:
   - General / OBC Category: INR 1,30,000 per semester
   - SC / ST Category: Full tuition fee waiver (as per GoI norms)
   - EWS Category: Subject to verification, partial/full waiver may apply

2. Other Fees (applicable to all categories):
   - Development Fee: INR 10,000 per semester
   - Examination Fee: INR 2,500 per semester
   - Student Activity Fee: INR 1,500 per semester
   - Internet and IT Infrastructure Fee: INR 3,000 per semester
   - Library Fee: INR 2,000 per semester
   - Medical Insurance Premium: INR 1,000 per year (charged in odd semesters)

3. Hostel and Mess Charges:
   - Hostel Room Rent: INR 12,000 per semester
   - Hostel Maintenance and Electricity: INR 8,000 per semester
   - Hostel Amenities Fee: INR 3,000 per semester
   - Mess Fee (estimated): INR 22,000 per semester (subject to mess committee review)
   - Mess Caution Deposit: INR 5,000 (one-time, refundable at the time of leaving)
   ** Total Hostel Fee (excluding mess): INR 23,000 per semester
   ** Total Hostel + Mess Fee: INR 45,000 per semester

4. One-Time Fees (at the time of admission):
   - Admission Fee: INR 5,000 (non-refundable)
   - Caution Money Deposit: INR 10,000 (refundable on leaving the institute)
   - Alumni Association Fee: INR 1,000 (one-time)

Payment Mode:
- All fees must be paid through SBI Collect portal before the start of each semester.
- Fee receipts must be submitted to the Accounts section for verification.
- Late payment attracts a penalty of INR 100 per day for the first 15 days, and INR 500 per day thereafter.

Scholarships and Financial Aid:
- Merit-cum-Means Scholarship: Available for students with family income below INR 8,00,000 per annum. Students must apply through the Dean (Student Affairs) office.
- AICTE Pragati Scholarship: For girl students in technical education.
- Institute Merit Scholarship: Top 10% students in each branch based on SGPA may receive a tuition fee waiver for the subsequent semester.

Fee Refund Policy (in case of withdrawal):
- Before the start of classes: Full refund minus INR 1,000 processing fee
- Within 15 days of classes: 80% refund of tuition fee
- Between 15–30 days: 50% refund of tuition fee
- After 30 days: No refund
