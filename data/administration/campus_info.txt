Campus Information – IIIT Una
==============================

Indian Institute of Information Technology Una (IIIT Una) is an Institute of National Importance established by the Ministry of Education (formerly MHRD), Government of India under the Public-Private Partnership (PPP) model.

Location:
The permanent campus of IIIT Una is situated at Saloh, Tehsil Haroli, District Una, Himachal Pradesh, PIN Code 177209.
The campus is approximately 15 km from Una town and well-connected by road via NH-503.
The nearest railway station is Una Himachal railway station (approximately 20 km).
The nearest airport is Gaggal Airport, Kangra (approximately 90 km).

Administrative Blocks:
- The main Administrative Block houses the Director's office, Registrar's office, Accounts section, and the Establishment section.
- Administrative inquiries regarding bonafide certificates, fee refund policies, institute identity cards, migration certificates, and transit passes are handled here.
- Office hours: 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday to Friday.
- For urgent matters, students may contact the Dean of Student Affairs office.

Anti-Ragging Committee:
IIIT Una has a zero-tolerance policy towards ragging. An Anti-Ragging Committee and Anti-Ragging Squad are constituted each year. Any incident of ragging must be reported to the committee immediately. Complaints can be lodged through the UGC Anti-Ragging Helpline (1800-180-5522) or the institute's internal grievance cell.
